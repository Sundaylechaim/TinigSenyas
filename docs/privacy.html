TinigSenyas Privacy Policy & Legal Disclosures

Effective Date: October 31, 2026
COPPA Mandatory Child Notice: TinigSenyas is strictly NOT intended for, directed to, or marketed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect, record, or store personal or biometric data from children under 13.

1. COPPA Compliance (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)

In compliance with 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506 and the FTC COPPA Rule, TinigSenyas enforces an age verification gate before granting access to interactive translation features (camera gesture tracking and microphone speech-to-text). Children under the age of 13 are prohibited from submitting personal data, audio recordings, or video frames. If we discover that personal data of a child under 13 has been collected without verifiable parental consent, such data will be purged immediately from our databases.

2. GDPR & Third-Party Data Disclosures

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), we adhere to the principles of data minimization and privacy by design:

  • Self-Hosted Resources: All visual assets, logos, and stylesheets on our website are self-hosted. We do not make unauthorized requests to external CDNs or Google Fonts that leak visitor IP addresses.
  • Zero Advertising/Tracking Pixels: We do not deploy Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, session replay scripts (Hotjar, FullStory), or behavioral ad networks.
  • External Links & Third Parties: External tools (such as GitHub, Appetize.io, or Sideloadly) are loaded solely upon explicit user action or click-through.
  • Optional Cloud Speech (TTS): If an online Filipino voice is configured, text is dispatched over encrypted HTTPS to the backend speech synthesizer solely for real-time audio playback.

3. CIPA & Wiretapping Protections (California Invasion of Privacy Act)

Pursuant to California Penal Code § 631 & § 632:

  • Microphone Audio: Audio from your device is captured strictly during active dictation sessions initiated by the user. Two-party consent is obtained prior to recording.
  • Camera Visual Processing: Video stream frames are processed on-device (or locally via MediaPipe) to compute hand landmarks. Video frames are not recorded or broadcast without permission.
  • Private Mode: Users may disable local conversation logging at any time in Settings.

4. CAN-SPAM Compliance (Commercial Messaging)

We do not engage in unsolicited mass email marketing. If users opt in to update alerts:

  • Every message includes a conspicuous physical postal address (2034 Zamora Street, Brgy. 782, Sta. Ana, Manila, Philippines) and identifiable sender headers.
  • Every message provides an automated, functioning 1-click unsubscribe link. Opt-outs are honored immediately and within 10 business days as required by law.

5. Recurring Charges & California Automatic Renewal Law (ARL)

TinigSenyas is free of charge. If optional premium features or memberships are introduced in the future:

  • Continuous service terms, pricing, and "renews until cancelled" notices will be shown prominently before purchase.
  • Affirmative consent will be mandatory before billing.
  • Frictionless online cancellation will be accessible directly in account settings.

6. DMCA & Copyright Contact

To report alleged copyright infringement, review our DMCA Takedown Policy or contact our designated agent at: dmca@tinigsenyas.org.
