How to install on Android:

  1. Tap Download Android APK above.
  2. Once downloaded, open the file from your Notifications or Downloads folder.

iOS Package (.IPA)
No Mac Required

Deploy directly to your iPhone without needing a paid $99/year Apple Developer account.

⬇️ Download TinigSenyas.ipa 🌐 Test Live in Browser (Appetize.io)

Method 1: Install on iPhone using Sideloadly (Free)

  1. Download Sideloadly on your PC/Mac from sideloadly.io.
  2. Connect your iPhone to your computer with a USB cable and tap Trust.
  3. Drag the downloaded TinigSenyas-iOS.ipa into Sideloadly.
  4. Enter your standard Apple ID email and click Start.
  5. On your iPhone: go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → tap your Apple ID → Trust.
  6. If prompted: Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode → ON.
No iPhone? You can download the Simulator ZIP and drop it into Appetize.io to run it interactively right in your web browser!
