1. Filing a Formal Takedown Notice (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3))

Your notification must include substantially the following:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the copyright interest.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed.
  3. A description of where the alleged infringing material is located within our application or repository (including specific URLs, filenames, or gesture labels).
  4. Your contact details: physical address, telephone number, and valid email address.
  5. A statement by you that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement by you, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

2. Counter-Notification Procedure (17 U.S.C. § 512(g))

If material you submitted was removed or disabled as a result of a DMCA notice, and you believe this was done by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification to our Designated Agent containing:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the material that has been removed or to which access has been disabled.
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, address, telephone number, and consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court.

3. Repeat Infringer Policy (17 U.S.C. § 512(i))

In accordance with Section 512(i) of the DMCA, TinigSenyas maintains and expeditiously enforces a policy that provides for the immediate termination of accounts and access privileges of users, contributors, or subscribers who repeatedly infringe copyright.
